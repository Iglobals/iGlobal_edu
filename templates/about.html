Inside the Materials

How We Compile the Books

Every resource in the iGlobals library answers one question: what does a student need to pass this exam? Here's how we build each one.

01 — Focus
Cut the noise

Focus on what gets you marks

Textbooks cover everything. Exams test only what matters. Our library is built around what shows up in JUPEB consistently, so you spend revision time on topics that count. Less noise, more marks.

02 — Practice
Worked, then yours to try

Learn through worked examples

See how it's done first. Understand the reasoning, the way answers are structured, what examiners look for. Then practice immediately while it's fresh. That's the difference between learning and forgetting.

03 — Retention
Built to stick

Built for Long-term Retention

Each chapter builds on the one before it. By the time you finish a topic, it connects to what you already understand. That's the difference between reading something and being able to use it under exam pressure.
